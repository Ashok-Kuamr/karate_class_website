The Master Behind the Dojo

Mr. Naresh Kumawat

Black Belt · National Level Karateka · Head Instructor

Sensei Naresh Kumawat is not just an instructor — he is a proven national-level competitor who has won multiple championships across several years. His journey in martial arts has shaped him into one of the most skilled and dedicated karate practitioners in the region.

With over 5 years of competitive and teaching experience, Sensei Naresh brings both technical mastery and a deep passion for empowering students of all ages — especially women and children — through the discipline of karate.

He specialises in physical fitness training, self-defence techniques, and building mental resilience. His special batches for women's safety have helped hundreds of women feel confident and secure.
